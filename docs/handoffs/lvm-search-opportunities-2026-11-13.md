## 1. Beginner lessons: what customers ask

- A Q&A page is titled "How long does it take to learn Mah Jongg?" [S1]. A 2026 article on the
  same question says: "After a lesson or two, most people feel comfortable enough to sit at a
  table and play" [S2].
- A Las Vegas instructor's beginner FAQ answers: "Will I actually play a game in my first
  lesson?", "How many people do I need to book a beginner lesson?", "Do I need to buy tiles or a
  National Mah Jongg League card first?", "Is this the same as the Mahjong solitaire game on my
  phone?" [S3].
- Summerlink (the Summerlin Council site) lists a class "Taught by Las Vegas Mahjong" [S12].
- Assumption: people learning on their own want to know they can join without bringing a group.

## 2. Private lessons: what customers ask

- A Las Vegas page answers: "How much does a private American Mahjong lesson in Las Vegas
  cost?", "How many people do I need for a private mahjong lesson?", "Do I need my own tiles or
  NMJL card?", "Where in Las Vegas are private lessons held?", "What if I have to reschedule?" [S4].
- A New York page answers: "Do I need experience?", "Can I play my own tiles?", "What if I need
  to cancel?", "What if we want to book a large group?" [S5].
- Assumption: people want to know the session length and cancellation terms before they write in.

## 3. Visiting players: what they need

- A forum poster asked whether Las Vegas has "scheduled games ... and how does one find out
  about them?" (Aug 7, 2004) [S6].
- A local roundup lists four groups playing four styles, only one of them American mahjong
  [S7], so "which mahjong?" matters. An open play listing in Phoenix states its level ("players
  who already know the basics of American Mahjong") and asks for "Your 2026 NMJL card (BYOC)" [S8].
- A Las Vegas tournament on March 7 to 9, 2027 sells hotel packages; its commuter tickets require
  living within 35 miles [S9].
- Assumptions: visitors on short trips may want evening or weekend seats; players in town for a
  tournament may look for games around their dates.

## 4. Corporate: what planners ask

- A Las Vegas corporate mahjong article answers: "How many people can a corporate mahjong event
  accommodate?", "What venues work for corporate mahjong events in Las Vegas?", "How much does a
  corporate mahjong event cost?", "Do attendees need to know how to play?", and tells planners to
  allow "a card-table footprint per four attendees" [S10].
- A Las Vegas team-building guide (updated Aug 7, 2026) compares activities by group size range,
  duration, cost band and "best for" [S11].
- Assumptions: planners shortlist on group size and lead time; hotels may ask outside vendors for
  an insurance certificate.

## Gaps on existing pages, ranked (as found on the live site)

Confirmed-fact fixes first, then by strength of evidence. No new page is justified: every need
fits an existing page.

1. `/mahjong-lessons-las-vegas` (need 1): never states the class length (about 2.5 hours), the
   group size (4 to 8) or that booking is online; all three "Book" buttons go to `/#classes`.
   [S3]
2. `/schedule` (need 3): every Social Open Play listing says "Drop in and play for two hours",
   contradicting "not drop-in hours" on `/play-mahjong-las-vegas` and "Required in advance" on
   `/mahjong-open-play-las-vegas`. [S6]
3. `/mahjong-lessons-summerlin`, `/mahjong-lessons-henderson` (need 1): pitch "we come to you"
   lessons; neither mentions MAHJ101 at the studio; buttons go to `/#classes`. [S3]
4. `/private-mahjong-lessons-las-vegas` (need 2): says "In home sessions are available by
   request" but leaves out "or venue"; session length, largest group and cancellation terms
   need the owner. [S4] [S5]
5. Learning time (need 1): "playing confidently after just one session" on the lessons page;
   "2-3 lessons" and "after their first session" on `/learn-mahjong`; "after just one" lesson in
   the homepage FAQ. [S1] [S2] [S3]
6. `/mahjong-lessons-las-vegas` FAQ (need 1): no "What do I need to bring?" or "Can I come on my
   own?". [S3]
7. Corporate hub, team building and meeting planner pages (need 4): no headcount range or lead
   time; room setup only as a passing "four to a table". [S10] [S11]
8. `/play-mahjong-las-vegas` (need 3): answers style, card, solo seats, price and reservations
   well; no answer for a visitor without a current card; all listed sessions are weekday
   mornings. [S8] [S9]
